🛡️ Safety Center & Meetup Guidelines

ResellBD is committed to making peer-to-peer resale in Bangladesh 100% secure. Follow these essential safety tips when trading:

1. Meet in Well-Lit Public Spaces

Always choose shopping malls, metro stations, police station perimeters, or crowded university areas for item handovers.

2. Inspect Hardware Thoroughly Before Paying

Check device screens, cameras, battery health, IMEI/serial numbers, and charger ports before handing over payment.

3. Never Send Advance Payments

Never send bKash/Nagad advance money for "courier fees" or "booking reservations" to unknown persons.
